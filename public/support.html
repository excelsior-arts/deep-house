Playing it

  • Press play in the middle of the ring. Space plays and pauses on a keyboard.
  • The dice rolls a new track. The arrows go to the next or the previous theme.
  • Move the eight sigils to change the music (drag them, or tap one on a phone): Ember is the tempo. Turn it up for liquid drum & bass, down for ambient soundscapes.
  • The gear opens the machine view: every voice, bus and meter of the track as it plays.
  • Every track has a link. Share it, and it plays the same anywhere.
