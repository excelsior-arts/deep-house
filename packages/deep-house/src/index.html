An astrolabe for music

A clockwork music machine. Roll the dice to wind it, set its eight sigils, and it plays an endless electronic set, every sound synthesized as the gears turn. At its best on three sounds: deep house, the default; liquid drum & bass (turn Ember up); ambient soundscapes (turn Ember down). A music companion for work, reading or a late drive. No distracting vocals. No agenda. Just vibes. One link, one track: share it and it plays the same anywhere. Fully offline. No account. No ads. No tracking.
